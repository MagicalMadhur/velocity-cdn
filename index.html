🚀

32-Thread Parallel Acceleration

Splits every file into concurrent byte-range requests. Assembles segments directly on disk with preallocated seek offsets to completely bypass single-socket throttling.

🎬

4K, 1440p & 8K Video Extraction

Browsers only give you 720p streams. Velocity uses embedded yt-dlp to capture hidden master-quality video files from YouTube, Vimeo, and video platforms worldwide.

🔊

Bundled FFmpeg & Loudness Norm

High-res video and audio streams are merged seamlessly on the fly. Applies FFmpeg loudnorm calibration to eliminate jarring volume spikes.

🎛️

Token-Bucket Rate Limiting

A true global rate limiter governs all active threads across all downloads. Cap your bandwidth consumption precisely so gaming and streaming stay buffer-free.

📁

Smart File Auto-Categorization

Automatically organizes downloads into Videos, Music, Documents, Images, Compressed Archives, and Software based on file extensions.

🧩

Seamless Browser Interception

Lightweight extension communicates with Velocity on local port 127.0.0.1:6800 to catch download clicks and inject overlay buttons directly onto video players.
